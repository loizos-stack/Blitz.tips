# Announcement: Supercapper prize pool $25,000 → $10,000

Send via **Admin → Emails**, audience **Contest entrants**.

This is a change to a guarantee people entered under, so it should go out as its
own message rather than riding along with the weekly recap digest — the digest
only reaches verified, opted-in entrants during the live phase, which is a
narrower list than everyone who entered.

**Subject:** A change to the Supercapper prize pool

**Body** (paste into the composer — it applies the Blitz.tips wrapper and
unsubscribe footer automatically):

---

Hi,

We're writing because something changed about the contest you entered, and you
should hear it from us directly rather than noticing it on the page.

The Supercapper guaranteed prize pool is moving from $25,000 to $10,000.

Nothing else about the contest changes. The scoring is the same, the rules are
the same, the payout structure is the same shape — the prize ladder scales
proportionally across all 20 paid places, so your position on the leaderboard is
worth the same share of the pool it was worth before. Every pick you've already
posted still stands and is still graded exactly as it was.

We'd rather run a smaller number we can honour than advertise one we can't. The
$25,000 figure was set before we had a clear read on entry numbers, and holding
to it would have meant either cutting the prize later — after picks were in and
positions were decided — or quietly changing the rules. Adjusting now, while the
board is still early, is the version of this that's fair to you.

If that changes your mind about competing, we understand, and you can withdraw
your entry from your dashboard at any time with no penalty.

Questions, or want to talk it through? Just reply to this email.

— The Blitz.tips team

---

## Notes

- **Audience gate:** the broadcast route only sends to users with
  `notifyEmail: true`. That is the right default for marketing, but a material
  change to a guarantee someone entered under is arguably a relationship
  message, not a promotion. If you want it to reach entrants who opted out of
  marketing email, that's a deliberate decision to make and a one-line change to
  the route — it isn't the current behaviour.
- **Timing:** send before the next weekly digest so the recap isn't the first
  place the new number appears.
- **Public pages** already show $10,000 (shipped in `20260726190000_contest_pool_10k`).
